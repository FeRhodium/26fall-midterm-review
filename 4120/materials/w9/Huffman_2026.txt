Full binary trees
• A binary tree in which every node has either zero or two children.
• The symbols are at the leaves, and each codeword is defined by a path from root to leaf,
  interpreting left as 0 and right as 1.
• Decoding is unique: a string of bits is decrypted by starting at the root, reading the string
  from left to right to move downward the tree (left as 0 and right as 1), and whenever a
  leaf is reached, outputting the corresponding symbol and returning to the root.

Conclusions
• Greedy algorithms are simple and intuitive.
• In many cases they are also optimal.
• If not optimal, we like to get an approximation ratio, i.e., a bound on how
  they compare to the optimum in the worse case.
