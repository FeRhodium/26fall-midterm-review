Conclusions
• Shortest paths from a single source exist when there are no negative
  loops; they form a shortest-path tree.
• The “value function” 𝛿 (weight of shortest path) at each node satisfy
  the Bellman equations (triangle inequality).
• 𝛿 satisfies a set of inequalities with at least one equality per node
  and can be solved by “relaxations” starting from some conservative
  estimate.
• If we could guess the shortest path tree, then 𝑉 − 1 relaxations
  could lead to the solution of 𝛿.
• Bellman-Ford algorithm: plays safe and relaxes all 𝑚 edges 𝑉 − 1
  times, with complexity Θ(𝑛𝑚).
