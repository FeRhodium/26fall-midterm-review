Conclusions
• When we care for the SP to a single destination Dijkstra may be inefficient
  since it explores the graph towards all possible directions.
• There are some smart ways to remedy that. We examined bi-directional
  search, and potential-based methods. These later ones modify Dijkstra’s
  algorithm to focus its search towards the destination.
• 𝐴∗ is an AI algorithm that finds shortest paths on the plane when obstacles
  are inserted. It uses a potential function ℎ that approximates the future
  costs from any given node.
• 𝐴∗ can be seen as a specific case of Dijkstra’s algorithm with weights that
  reflect future costs.
