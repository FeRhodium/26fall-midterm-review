Conclusions
 • Graphs: fundamental data structure
   • Directed and undirected
   • 4 possible representations
 • Basic methods of graph search: BFS and DFS
 • BFS: useful to find reachable nodes
   • Constructs the “reachability” or “shortest path” tree: each node
     knows who is the next node on his shortest path (minimum hop
     count) towards the root node
